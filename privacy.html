Outreach Pipeline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Outreach Pipeline is a personal-use application operated by the owner of nayyirsahsan@gmail.com. It is not offered to other users.

Gmail access and use

With the owner's authorization, the application uses Gmail read access to identify replies, delivery failures, existing drafts, and sent messages relevant to outreach. It uses Gmail compose access to create and send owner-approved outreach messages. It does not send automatic replies or delete mail.

Data stored

The application stores prospect names, professional contact details, research sources, draft subjects and bodies, approval and suppression states, and limited message identifiers and timestamps in the owner's Airtable workspace. A private ledger, OAuth credentials, and maintenance logs are stored on the owner's Mac. Gmail message bodies are inspected when needed to verify an outgoing message, but are not copied into Airtable outcome records.

Other services

Airtable hosts the owner's outreach records. Google provides Gmail and, when the owner requests draft preparation, Gemini model processing. Healthchecks.io receives only maintenance success or failure pings; those pings contain no Gmail content or credentials. The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Control, retention, and deletion

The owner controls whether sending is enabled, may revoke Gmail access in Google Account settings, and may delete local credentials, the local ledger, and Airtable records. Records are retained until the owner removes them; there is no automatic deletion schedule. For questions or removal requests, contact nayyirsahsan@gmail.com.

Policy changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
